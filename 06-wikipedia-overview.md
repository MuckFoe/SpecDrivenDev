# Wikipedia — "Spec-driven development" / "Specification-driven development"

## Definition
"Specification-driven development is a software development approach in which specifications are used to develop software." Classified under documentation-driven development methodologies.

## Lineage (pre-LLM usage of the term)
Earliest cited reference: Ostroff, Makalsky & Paige (2004), describing an **agile** approach that combines:
- **Test-driven development** (tests as one kind of specification), with
- **Design by contract** (contracts as another kind of specification).

Related to model-driven development, model transformation, and round-trip engineering.

## Research note
This confirms the term "spec-driven development" **predates** the 2025 LLM-era usage by roughly two decades in an unrelated (TDD + design-by-contract) sense. The 2025 AI-coding usage (Spec Kit, Kiro, Tessl, Grove's talk) is a distinct, newer meaning of the same name — worth flagging when citing "SDD" externally, since the term is overloaded.
